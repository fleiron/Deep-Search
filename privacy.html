Privacy Policy for “AI Deep Search: Relationships”

Last updated: August 2026

Owner & developer: Indie developer (contact: ilushazs@icloud.com)

1. Introduction

Thank you for using AI Deep Search: Relationships (the “App”, “DeepSearch”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use the App.

The App helps you analyze conversation screenshots with AI. You upload chat screenshots, optionally add a little context, and receive a structured relationship / communication report. The App is not a cheating detector, not legal advice, and not a diagnosis.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Related terms, including auto-renewing subscriptions, are described in our Terms of Use.

2. Data We Collect

The App works without a password login. We do not ask for your real legal name, email address, or phone number to use the App. When you continue past onboarding, we create a lightweight App profile so we can apply usage limits and restore purchases.

2.1. Anonymous Identifiers & App Profile

To operate the App, quotas, and purchases, we use:

  • An anonymous device identifier stored securely in the iPhone Keychain and also stored on our servers to operate your App profile.
  • An 8-digit public ID shown in Profile (for support) and stored on our servers. This is not your phone number.
  • An optional nickname stored on this iPhone and on our servers, and an optional avatar kept as a local profile choice.
  • A matching RevenueCat App User ID (the same anonymous device identifier), used to link purchases to your device / App Store account.
  • Apple DeviceCheck device eligibility for the one-time free analysis (stored by Apple; not a nickname, report, or conversation record).
  • Standard Apple in-app purchase identifiers (product identifiers and transaction information generated by Apple).

These identifiers do not include your real name, email address, or phone number by default.

2.2. Conversation Screenshots & Optional Context (AI Analysis)

When you run an analysis, you choose screenshots from your Photo Library using Apple’s system picker (PhotosPicker). The App does not request full Photo Library access and does not browse your camera roll. You may also type optional extra context (for example, “we’ve been dating two months”). You also select a relationship type (Dating, Relationship, Crush, Ex, or Other) and which side of the chat is yours.

Only the images and text you explicitly submit for that analysis are processed. Submitting screenshots is processing of user content. It is sent to our servers and then to OpenAI even though DeepSearch does not keep the files after the request finishes.

2.3. What We Send to OpenAI

After you confirm the in-app consent screen, the App sends the following to DeepSearch’s servers, and our servers then send it to OpenAI (currently via OpenAI’s API) to generate your report:

  • The screenshots you selected (resized/compressed for processing).
  • Any extra context you typed.
  • The relationship type and which messages are yours, so the model can read the conversation from the correct side.
  • A language / locale hint so the report can be written in a matching language.

OpenAI is used only to produce that report. We do not use your screenshots or conversation content to train our own models, to show ads, or to identify people in the chat. We do not opt in to use API inputs to train OpenAI models. We send analysis requests with OpenAI’s store flag disabled so OpenAI does not keep Responses API application state for later retrieval.

If the images are not a readable chat (for example, nature photos), OpenAI may still receive them in order to return an “unreadable” / “no conversation” result. Those attempts still count toward anti-abuse limits, but a failed analysis is not counted as a successful free/Premium analysis.

2.4. What DeepSearch Does Not Keep After Processing

After the AI response is returned to your iPhone, DeepSearch does not keep on our servers:

  • raw screenshots or image files;
  • OCR text or reconstructed chat transcripts;
  • the extra context you typed;
  • the full report JSON.

On our servers, screenshots are processed in memory and discarded. They are not written to disk and are not stored in our database.

OpenAI is a separate processor. Even with store disabled, OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs for a limited period (typically up to 30 days) under OpenAI’s own terms and API data usage policies (for example, abuse monitoring). We do not control that schedule. OpenAI API data is not used to train OpenAI models by default unless the API customer opts in. See also OpenAI’s Privacy Policy.

2.5. What Stays on Your iPhone

The generated report and your analysis history are saved on this device only. Uninstalling the App removes that local history (app files). Delete Account also replaces the Keychain identifier with a new one. After uninstall, a leftover Keychain identifier may remain until iOS removes it. We cannot recover a deleted on-device report.

2.6. Account Metadata Stored on Our Servers

To run the App we store limited metadata, for example:

  • device identifier, public ID, nickname, optional avatar;
  • approximate country / region inferred from standard request headers and device locale (not GPS, not precise location; we do not store your IP address);
  • app version and platform (iOS);
  • usage counters (for example, whether the free analysis was used on this App profile, analyses today);
  • a premium cache based on RevenueCat / Apple;
  • minimal analysis logs: screenshot count, relationship type, success/failure code, model name, token counts, latency, and overall signal. No image and no report body.

2.7. Product Analytics

We record first-party product events such as opening tabs, starting an analysis, viewing the paywall, or updating a nickname. Those events may include non-content properties such as screenshot count, relationship type, or a paywall source. They never include screenshots, message text, or extra context.

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, do not show ads, and do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. The App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track for advertising.

2.8. Purchase & Subscription Data

When you buy, start a free trial (if offered), or restore Premium, Apple and RevenueCat may process:

  • Product identifier (Weekly or Yearly Premium).
  • Purchase date, trial/intro status, renewal status, and expiration.
  • App Store country or region.
  • Refund or cancellation status, if Apple provides it.

We do not see or store your full payment card details. Payments are processed by Apple via the App Store. Prices, trial length (if any), and renewal cost are shown in the App and on Apple’s purchase sheet before you confirm. See our Terms of Use for auto-renewal details.

2.9. Information You Provide Voluntarily

If you email us at ilushazs@icloud.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use it only to respond.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the analysis you requested, via our backend and OpenAI.
  • To return the report to your iPhone and let you keep history locally.
  • To enforce free and Premium limits fairly, including one complimentary analysis per device via Apple DeviceCheck, and to limit abusive repeat attempts that would otherwise send large volumes of images to OpenAI.
  • To verify and restore purchases through Apple and RevenueCat.
  • To understand product usage (without conversation content) and keep the App reliable.
  • To respond to support emails and honor deletion / access requests where reasonably possible.

4. Consent for AI Data Sharing

Before the first analysis, the App shows a consent screen that explains that selected screenshots and optional context will be sent to OpenAI. Analysis does not start until you confirm.

You can revoke this consent anytime in the App: Profile → AI Data Sharing → Revoke. After you revoke, the App will not send screenshots to OpenAI until you agree again on the next analysis. Revoking consent does not automatically delete past on-device reports or already-processed API logs held by OpenAI under its policies.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1. Apple App Store & In-App Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. Apple handles payment data under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not control Apple’s processing of App Store payments.

5.2. RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage entitlements and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives the anonymous App User ID and purchase-related information. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

5.3. Our Backend Servers

The App talks to DeepSearch API servers to bootstrap your identity, check usage, record product events, and run analysis. Analysis requests include the screenshots and context described above. After OpenAI returns a report, image bytes and conversation content are discarded on our server.

5.4. OpenAI

We use OpenAI as the AI provider that reads the submitted screenshots and writes the structured report. Content required for that generation is transmitted securely for processing. OpenAI processes data under its terms and privacy policy applicable to API customers. If we replace the AI provider in the future, we will update this Policy.

5.5. Apple DeviceCheck

DeepSearch uses Apple DeviceCheck to prevent repeated redemption of the one-time free analysis. This device-level eligibility state does not contain conversation content, reports, nickname, or profile information. Apple stores that two-bit state; DeepSearch does not keep a persistent device fingerprint in our database after you delete your account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share screenshots or chat content with advertisers. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising (including as “sale” or “sharing” under the CCPA/CPRA).

We may share limited data with:

  • OpenAI, solely to generate the report you requested;
  • RevenueCat and Apple, solely to operate purchases;
  • Apple DeviceCheck, solely to remember whether this device already redeemed the one-time free analysis;
  • infrastructure providers that host our backend;
  • authorities if required by applicable law.

7. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the App, analysis, and Premium you request.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the App secure, prevent abuse, enforce quotas, and operate subscriptions.
  • Consent: we ask before sending screenshots and optional context to OpenAI. You can withdraw that consent in Profile.

8. Data Retention

  • Screenshots, OCR, extra context, and full reports are not retained on DeepSearch servers after processing.
  • OpenAI may retain API inputs/outputs for a limited time (typically up to 30 days) under OpenAI’s policies, even when we disable Responses API storage.
  • On-device reports remain until you delete the App, delete your account in Profile, or clear that local history.
  • Account metadata, usage counters, analytics events, and purchase/entitlement records are kept as needed to provide the App and meet legal/accounting obligations, or until you request deletion.
  • Apple DeviceCheck eligibility for the one-time free analysis is stored by Apple for that device and is not a DeepSearch profile record.
  • Support emails are kept as needed to handle your request.

9. Access, Correction & Deletion

You can change nickname and avatar in Profile. You can revoke AI data sharing in Profile. On-device reports are removed if you delete the App or delete your account.

In-app account deletion: go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes, from DeepSearch:

  • your App profile (device identifier mapping, public ID, nickname, avatar);
  • usage counters and analysis metadata;
  • product analytics events tied to that profile;
  • reports and history stored on this iPhone;
  • the RevenueCat subscriber record we can delete for that anonymous ID, when available.

After deletion, the App returns you to onboarding and creates a new anonymous Keychain identifier. A new server profile is created only if you continue using the App. This cannot be undone. You may also email ilushazs@icloud.com with your 8-digit public ID if you cannot use the in-app control.

Account deletion does not delete:

  • an active Apple subscription (cancel in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions);
  • purchase records Apple is required to keep;
  • data already processed and retained by OpenAI under OpenAI’s policies;
  • Apple DeviceCheck eligibility that this device already redeemed the one-time free analysis (fraud prevention; it is not a profile and contains no screenshots, reports, nickname, or conversation content).

If you still have Premium after deletion, use Restore Purchases after you continue so Apple can attach it to the new profile.

10. Data Security

API traffic uses HTTPS. Screenshots are handled in memory on our server for the duration of the request. No method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for users 17 years of age or older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the equivalent age required by local law). Do not upload screenshots of children. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can delete associated metadata where reasonably possible.
